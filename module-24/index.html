The Situation

Listen Closely

A customer just opened a chat and sent this message:

"I've already contacted support twice about this and nobody seems to know what's happening."

As an agent, it's easy to jump straight to fixing the technical issue. But let's break down what they are actually saying first.

Step 1: The Fact

What actually happened?

Strip away the emotion. What is the core, objective fact here?

The customer is extremely angry.
They contacted support twice with no resolution.
Our technical system is completely broken.
Step 2: The Emotion

How might they feel?

We can't know for certain, but we can recognize the signals. Pick two likely emotions.

Frustrated
Grateful
Curious
Unheard
Step 3: The Need

What do they need from you?

Fill in the missing pieces. Besides a fix, what else does the customer need right now?

Need 1...
Need 2...
A generic apology
Someone to take ownership
A clear explanation
An immediate refund
Takeaway

You heard them!

The teaching point: The strongest answer is not simply "the customer needs the problem fixed."

The customer may certainly need a resolution, but at this point they also need confidence that someone understands the situation and is taking responsibility for moving it forward.[cite: 1]
