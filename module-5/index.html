🌸 The 5 Questions Before You Solve

Welcome, Agent! Before offering a solution, click each card to reveal the core questions you must ask yourself.

1️⃣ What is the customer actually experiencing?
Not what I think is happening. What do I *know*?
2️⃣ What info am I missing?
What unanswered question could change my diagnosis?
3️⃣ What have I verified?
Separate hard facts from assumptions.
4️⃣ What is the most likely root cause?
Use evidence—not instinct alone.
5️⃣ Have I confirmed the solution?
Don't assume the problem is fixed. Ask!

⏸️ The "Pause Before Solve" Technique

One of the simplest habits an agent can develop is a diagnostic pause.

Before providing a solution, mentally stop for a moment and ask:
"Do I actually know what caused this?"

If the answer is no, keep investigating. This tiny pause prevents:

  • Incorrect solutions
  • Repeat contacts
  • Unnecessary escalations
  • Customer frustration
  • Incorrect documentation
