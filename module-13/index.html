📝 Quick Practice: Reading the Customer

Read the customer message below, type your thoughts, and check the QA tips to see how you did.

🧠 Customer Message:

"I've contacted support twice already, and no one has fixed this."

📌 1. What emotion is the customer expressing?
QA Tip: The customer is expressing extreme frustration, impatience, and feeling ignored. They are losing trust in the support team.
📌 2. What information have they already shared?
QA Tip: They have established a timeline: this is their third touchpoint ("twice already"). The core issue is still unresolved.
📌 3. What should you avoid repeating?
QA Tip: Avoid asking them to repeat basic details they likely provided in the first two interactions. Do not offer basic Level 1 troubleshooting steps unless absolutely necessary.
📌 4. What additional information would help you solve the problem?
QA Tip: You need to look up their previous ticket numbers to find out what was previously attempted, what the actual error/issue is, and why it failed to be resolved.
📌 5. How could you acknowledge their experience before offering a solution?
QA Tip: Use strong empathy. Example: "I completely understand your frustration—having to reach out three times for the same issue is incredibly exhausting. I am pulling up your previous tickets right now to get this sorted out for you immediately."
