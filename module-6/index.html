🛸 Cosmic Knowledge Command

Mastering Customer KB vs. Internal Knowledge Base (IKB)

🌐 Customer Article: "Damaged Product — Instructions"

Purpose: Informs the customer on what action they need to take on their end.

  • Instructions on where to submit damage claims.
  • List of proof needed (clear photos of shipping label & damage).
  • Timeframe limits for filing reports (e.g., within 14 days of delivery).
🔒 Internal Article: "Damaged Product — Agent Handling"

Purpose: Instructs the agent on execution steps behind the scenes.

  • Verify uploaded photo specs in the logistics system.
  • Auto-approve instant replacement if total value is under $100.
  • Escalate to Claims Command Level 2 if item contains high-value hazardous units.
  • System update codes required to log damage reasons accurately.
🛡️
Shield Confidential Logic

Never copy-paste internal system error codes or escalation rules to the customer. Translate actions into helpful guidance.

⚙️
Execution vs. Guidance

External KBs explain what the customer sees/does. Internal IKBs explain what systems you update and rules you apply.

🚀
Prevent Escalation Friction

Always verify IKB criteria before escalating—many issues can be resolved instantly at level 1 if requirements are met.
