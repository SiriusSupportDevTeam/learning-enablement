📌 Activity: Quick Reflection

Practice critical thinking: gather facts first before making decisions.

🏢 Scenario "I want a refund." (No reason provided)

🧠 1. Questions to Ask?

  • Why are you requesting a refund?
  • Which booking/order is this for?
  • When did it occur? What happened?
  • Spoken with another support agent?
  • Problem with product or service?
Goal: Gather facts to understand situation.

📜 2. Info Needed First

  • Customer account info
  • Booking/order & payment status
  • Company refund policy
  • Previous support notes
⚠️ Deciding without this leads to mistakes or inconsistent service.

💡 Example Breakdown

❌ Poor Approach
Processes refund immediately with no questions.
Result: Ignores policy/real issue.
✅ Better Approach
Asks questions & reviews policy before deciding.
Result: Accurate, fair, and factual.

🛑 Think Before You Act

📝 Key Reflection
"What do I need to know before I can make the right decision?"
