Field Guide 03

Confirmation Bias

Confirmation bias is the tendency to favor evidence that supports your first theory — and dismiss evidence that contradicts it. The Verify stage exists specifically to catch this.

Signs You're Biased

  • You stop asking questions once you have a theory that "feels right."
  • You reinterpret contradicting evidence to still fit your first guess.
  • You apply the same fix repeatedly, assuming it just needs to "take."

How Verify Counters It

  • Explicitly restate the suspected cause and ask the customer to confirm.
  • Look for one piece of evidence that would disprove your theory, not just support it.
  • Verify root cause even when a fix works on the first try — a lucky fix can mask the real issue.
Field Guide 04

When to Escalate

Escalation isn't failure — it's routing the issue to whoever can actually resolve it.

Outside your access

The fix requires tools, permissions, or systems you can't reach.

Likely systemic

Multiple customers report the same symptom around the same time.

Safety, security, or compliance risk

Any hint of data exposure, safety concern, or policy violation.

Framework exhausted

You've run Observe through Test with no resolution and impact is significant.
