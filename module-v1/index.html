Investigative Support Principles
Scenario 1: Missing Package [1]
Process: Explore, Clarify, Investigate [1, 2]
Lesson: Never confuse customer conclusion with verified information [2].
Scenario 2: "Broken" Website [3]
Process: Investigative Questioning [3]
Lesson: Never challenge the customer's conclusion. Investigate it [4].
Scenario 3: The Angry Customer [4]
Process: Acknowledge & Plan [4, 5]
Lesson: Demonstrate ownership and reduce repetition [5].
