Structured Logic

The 3-Layer Search Burger

Don't stop at the generic answer! Build the complete answer by clicking through all three layers of search logic.

LAYER 1: What is the Problem? (Top Bun)
Customer Description: "Missing component"
Action: Identify the core issue without process assumptions.
LAYER 2: What Process Applies? (Lettuce & Fixings)
Process SOP: "Missing component replacement process"
Action: Locate the general resolution protocol.
LAYER 3: What Exception Applies? (The Meat)
Special Scenario: "Retailer purchase + discontinued product"
Action: Verify eligibility rules, retail exceptions, and escalation triggers before finalizing your action!
