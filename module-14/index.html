OPERATIONAL PROTOCOL

The 6-Step Resolution Framework

Click any stage in the workflow sequence to review standard agent expectations:

Step 01
1. FIND
Locate potentially relevant information using reframed search queries.
Step 02
2. FOLLOW
Follow related articles, links, procedures, and information trails.
Step 03
3. FILTER
Remove information that does not apply to the specific customer scenario.
Step 04
4. VERIFY
Confirm that the remaining information is current and approved.
Step 05
5. RESOLVE
Apply the correct information to the customer's situation.
Step 06
6. FEED BACK
Document knowledge gaps, outdated content, or useful discoveries.
💡 Pro Tip: Never skip Step 6 (FEED BACK)! If you discovered a workaround or missing policy step, documenting it prevents the next agent from repeating the same investigation.

CRITICAL THINKING EXERCISES

"What Would You Do?" Scenarios

Formulate your reasoning in your head before clicking to reveal the expected response.

Scenario 1 — The Perfect Article

You find an article whose title exactly matches the customer's issue. However, the article is two years old.

Expected Response: Do not automatically use it. Check whether a newer approved procedure exists and verify whether the information remains current.

Scenario 2 — The Conflicting Tickets

Three recent tickets show three different resolutions for the same issue.

Expected Response: Do not choose the majority answer. Investigate the current approved policy/SOP and determine whether the cases had different circumstances or exceptions.

Scenario 3 — No Results

You search several variations of the customer's issue and find nothing.

Expected Response: Reframe the problem, identify organizational terminology, search related processes, check approved resources, and escalate if the information genuinely cannot be verified.

Scenario 4 — The Customer's Answer

The customer tells you: "Another agent said this is allowed."

Expected Response: Treat the information as useful context, not as proof. Verify the current approved procedure.
